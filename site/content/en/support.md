## Setting up and running

Most questions are answered by [Setup]({{< relref "setup" >}}), especially [Troubleshooting]({{< relref "setup/troubleshooting" >}}).

## Bugs, wishes and questions

Everything about development goes through the [issues on GitHub]({{< param "github" >}}/issues). For a bug it helps to give the version (in the app under *Account → About Hiwiski*), the platform, what you did, what you expected and what happened instead.

## Security issues

Please do not report them in a public issue, but through **Report a vulnerability** on the *Security* tab on [GitHub]({{< param "github" >}}/security).
