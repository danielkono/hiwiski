A page of the kind *Pinboard* has, below its text, a surface for everything that belongs to a plan: notes, photos, links to lists and pages, members of the family and whole calendar weeks — connected by labelled lines and drawn on with a pen. Open it full screen, move around with your fingers, the trackpad or the mouse wheel, and zoom in and out.

{{< scene name="pinboard" live=true height=600 >}}Try it: the sample family's summer holiday on Sardinia — places they wish for, to-dos, shopping and two weeks of dates. Hover over an event, or tap **Edit**.{{< /scene >}}

## What goes on a pinboard

- **Text** is typed right where it stands and is just as large as its words.
- **Notes** are written in Markdown. Their text stands top, middle or bottom, left, centre or right, and its lines follow the shape if you like — round in an ellipse, pointed in a diamond. A note's checklist is ticked off with a tap while you read the pinboard.
- **Pictures** keep their shape; several chosen at once stand side by side, as high as each other.
- **Links** show their target's title or its content — a list to tick off, a page to read.
- **Members of the family** show as their picture in a circle.
- **Calendar weeks** show one or more calendars side by side: this week, moving on with the days, or a week of the year. An event opens into its card on hover, as in the calendar. Whoever cannot see a calendar sees nothing of it there.

## Look

Elements get a colour — one of the eight, or your own, see-through if you like — a text colour, a shape and a text size. The knob above a selected element turns it, and where elements overlap you bring them forward or send them back.

**Connections** come as a line, an arrow, a double arrow, dashed or dotted, in a colour and thickness you choose first. Drag from one element to another, or tap one after the other, and go on from there; a connection can carry a label.

With the **pen** you draw freehand or a rectangle, ellipse, triangle, diamond, line or arrow; a shape can be resized later. The **eraser** takes away every stroke you swipe it over, and one tap hides the drawings.

## Arranging

A lasso or a rectangle selects several elements, to move, colour or delete them together — or to align them, line them up side by side or one below the other a gap apart, spread them out evenly or give them one width, height or size. What you move or resize snaps to the edges, middles, gaps and sizes around it, with a thin guide, or to a grid if you like; on a computer, holding ⌘ or Ctrl while dragging places freely. A new picture, note, link or member is as large as the last of its kind.

While **editing**, everything lies in three buttons in the bottom right corner — add, draw, tools — whose choices come out onto a ring you turn to see more. **Undo** and **Redo** take changes back and do them again. Tap twice and drag up or down to zoom with one finger.

## Together and offline

Several people can work on the same pinboard at once, offline too. Each element is synced on its own: when two people changed the same element at the same time, the pinboard shows your version beside the one everyone sees, and you decide for that element alone.

A search on the pinboard marks what it finds and lists it — as it lies on the pinboard, alphabetically or by date. Links on a pinboard count as links from its page, and the search finds the page by the words on its pinboard. **Save as picture** saves the whole pinboard as a PNG, and a page's export can take its pinboards along as pictures.
