## Across everything

Events, tasks, lists, pages and recipes. The matching passage is highlighted, with where it was found below it. Filters by type and tag. "kinder" also finds "Kinderarzt".

{{< scene name="search" live=true height=520 >}}Try it: search for "grandma".{{< /scene >}}

## Numbers

Every item has a short number such as `TD-14` or `WIKI-3`. Type one, and search jumps straight there. The number does not change.

## Good to know

- Offline the app searches what is on the device and shows that it does.
- Only what you may see is found.
- The archive is left out unless you pick it.
